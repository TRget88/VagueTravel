ICW Nav Aid

Red on the right, until you turn around.

A card sized reminder of which side the channel markers belong on. Two lights, one button to flip them, and nothing to keep in your head.

Why it exists

It started with my dad.

He was keeping track of channel marker orientation with navigation aids that were easy to mix up. On the Intracoastal Waterway, the side a marker belongs on depends on which way you are headed.

So this is the simplest fix I could build. A toggle that takes almost no effort to read.

How it works

One press swaps the sides.

Press POWER to turn the card on. The light on each side shows the color of the marks on that side. When your direction changes, press FLIP and the two colors trade places.

Left green, right red.

The cover

A soft pouch to keep it in.

The card will ship in a soft plastic envelope pouch. The design is still being finished.

The battery

One coin cell, no app.

A single CR2032 coin cell powers the card. There is nothing to charge and nothing to pair.

What is inside

Every part on one side.

One board the size of a credit card, a coin cell, two lights and two buttons. That is the whole thing.

The white cover in this model is the 3D printed version. It is not going away, and the files are in the library if you want to print your own.
