ICW Nav Aid

Red on the right, until you turn around.

A card sized reminder of which side the channel markers belong on. Two lights, one button to flip them, and nothing to keep in your head.

See the design on Nurbly

Scroll to take it apart

Why it exists

It started with my dad.

He was keeping track of channel marker orientation with navigation aids that were easy to mix up. On the Intracoastal Waterway, the side a marker belongs on depends on which way you are headed.

So this is the simplest fix I could build. A toggle that takes almost no effort to read.

How it works

One press swaps the sides.

POWER latches the card on. The light on each side shows the color of the marks on that side. When your direction changes, press FLIP and the two colors trade places.

Left green, right red.

The cover

A white shell that glows.

The cover is 3D printed in white PLA or PETG. Above each LED the plate thins to a 0.6 mm skin, and a small tube around the LED keeps the light in a clean circle.

It snaps onto the board past four small ledges.

The battery

One coin cell, no app.

A single CR2032 powers the card from a surface mount holder under the cover. There is nothing to charge and nothing to pair.

To change the cell, press the board out of the cover from behind.

What is inside

Every part on one side.

A single layer board the exact size of a credit card, with all the parts on the front.
